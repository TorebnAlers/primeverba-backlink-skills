# PrimeVerba Quality Discovery & SEO

Use this skill for PrimeVerba directory discovery, listing preparation, backlink-quality assessment, and SEO writing.

## Purpose and limits

Optimise for relevant discovery, accurate product information, qualified referral traffic, useful content, and durable listings. Do not optimise for backlink counts, Dofollow links, DA/DR, PageRank, rankings, or submission volume.

Never use bulk link spam, paid link placements, reciprocal-link schemes, keyword-rich anchors, private blog networks, fake identities, fabricated reviews, CAPTCHA bypass, proxy evasion, mass outreach, guest-post pitching, or automatic submissions.

This is a PrimeVerba-specific adaptation inspired by the guardrails in this MIT-licensed repository. It does not treat any bundled candidate list as current or approved.

## Source of truth

Before work, load the current approved PrimeVerba product facts, product-claim evidence, website privacy/terms pages, and the planned article cluster. Use verified facts only. Never invent features, pricing, customers, performance, integrations, legal promises, processing geography, or compatibility.

## Mandatory humanizer pass

Every public-facing text must undergo a final humanizer edit after factual and SEO review. Keep verified claims, links, caveats, and search intent intact while removing canned AI phrasing, empty hype, vague attributions, formulaic sections, and generic conclusions. The voice should be direct, useful, and restrained.

## Directory discovery: read-only first

1. Create a task card with target audience, markets, product type, canonical URL, approved assets, exact facts, exclusions, and date.
2. Find candidates by audience and product fit, not SEO metrics.
3. Inspect relevance, user/discovery value, governance, listing quality, availability, terms, data collection, fees, reciprocal demands, link-selling, and route fit.
4. Exclude irrelevant, low-quality, templated, link-selling, forced-reciprocal, pay-to-rank, unavailable, or unverifiable candidates.
5. Build a pilot of no more than ten candidates with evidence URLs and a pass/fail reason.
6. Do not open accounts, enter forms, upload assets, accept terms, or submit in research mode.

## External action gate

Account registration, agreement acceptance, upload, payment, publication, outreach, and submission require the operator's explicit approval for the exact site and action immediately before execution.

When approved, recheck eligibility, terms, duplicate state, product facts, and approval. Use only the official brand name and canonical URL or naked URL as link text. Keep optional marketing subscriptions off. Do not retry an ambiguous final submission. Record safe evidence only: candidate, URL, timestamp, action, outcome, public listing URL, link rel when observable, and next step. Never record credentials, OTPs, tokens, cookies, raw contact data, or magic links.

## SEO content workflow

1. Select one topic and infer search intent conservatively.
2. Prepare a brief: reader, primary/supporting keywords, claim ledger, internal links, CTA, and publication status.
3. Verify every non-trivial claim; soften or remove uncertain claims.
4. Draft restrained English with title, meta description, article or outline, internal links, CTA, and FAQ where useful.
5. Check intent match, H1/title, first paragraph, headings, natural keyword use, reader risk notes, and metadata.
6. Run the mandatory humanizer pass.
7. Deliver a review-ready draft; do not publish or alter a website without separate approval.

## Success measures

Prioritise qualified referral visits, conversions, listing accuracy, listing survival, useful search impressions, and reader usefulness. Never use submission volume, backlink count, Dofollow placement, or domain metrics as proof of SEO success.
